# Expert System for RA 9208 (Anti-Trafficking in Persons Act of 2003)

An intelligent rule-based system designed to assist in identifying and classifying violations under **Republic Act 9208** of the Philippines, also known as the **Anti-Trafficking in Persons Act of 2003**. This project provides automated decision support for determining whether a scenario falls under:

- **Trafficking in Persons**
- **Promotion of Trafficking**
- **Qualified Trafficking**
- **Breach of Confidentiality**

---

## 📌 Overview

This Expert System simulates legal reasoning using rule-based logic. Given user inputs describing an incident, the system evaluates the facts and maps them to key provisions of RA 9208. It helps students, researchers, and law-oriented developers understand how trafficking-related offenses are identified.

> ⚠️ **Note:** This is an educational tool and is *not* a substitute for legal advice.

---

## ⚖️ Covered Legal Concepts

### **1. Trafficking in Persons**
Includes recruitment, transport, transfer, harboring, or receipt of persons through means such as threats, coercion, fraud, abuse of power, or giving payments to achieve control over another person for purposes of exploitation.

### **2. Promotion of Trafficking**
Acts that assist, support, or enable trafficking, such as:
- Providing travel documents
- Advertising false recruitment
- Operating establishments that facilitate trafficking
- Directing individuals toward trafficking activities

### **3. Qualified Trafficking**
Aggravated forms of trafficking, such as when:
- The victim is a child
- The crime is committed by a syndicate or large scale
- The offender is a parent, guardian, or authority figure
- The victim suffers serious injury or death

### **4. Breach of Confidentiality**
Unauthorized disclosure of information related to a trafficking case, including identity, case details, or personal information of victims.

## 🧠 System Features

- **Rule-Based Inference Engine:** Uses IF–THEN logic to classify offenses.
- **Scenario-Based Input:** User-friendly prompts for entering case details.
- **Explanation Component:** Shows which rules were triggered and why.
- **Modular Knowledge Base:** Organized by offense type for easier updates.
- **Educational Orientation:** Designed for legal informatics.
